# Doors: A Exit West Game
## Introduction

A text-based choose-your-own-adventure game based on Exit West by Mohsin Hamid. You play as Saeed, a young man. You meet Nadia, a free-spirted woman. Soon, your hometown starts to crumble. You hear rumours about magical doors, doors that will take you far away. You have to make difficult choices along your journey, ones that will determine your fate.

## Instructions:
- 1,2 for choices
- Restart button to restart
